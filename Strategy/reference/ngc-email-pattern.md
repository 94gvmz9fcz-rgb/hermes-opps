# NGC (Northrop Grumman) Email Pattern

**Pattern:** `firstname.lastname@ngc.com`

**Confidence:** High — not 100% fail-safe, but high upside/chance of accuracy.

**Source:** Josh Stewart — learned from working NGC extensively at CyKor.

**⚠️ ALWAYS cross-reference with actual CRM record before suggesting email updates.** 
The user may have changed a contact's company since the data was last synced.

## Do NOT apply to:

- **Serco / Cerco employees** — email pattern is different (Amy Nugent is Serco, not NGC)
- Anyone whose company field in Airtable doesn't contain NGC/Northrop

## Usage

- Retroactively fill in missing emails for existing NGC CRM contacts AFTER confirming company hasn't changed
- Add emails when adding people of interest → CRM
- Useful engagement tool: if an NGC contact comes on our radar and we're trying to figure out how to reach them, this pattern gives us a strong first guess
- Always verify if possible (send a test, check LinkedIn, etc.)
